Who we are

ShieldSpan is published by M.B. RENT IMOB S.R.L. (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the ShieldSpan app for iPhone, iPad and Android. You can reach us at hodkins@mbrentimob.com.

How filtering works

To block ads in every app, ShieldSpan uses the system VPN interface — Network Extension on iOS and iPadOS, VpnService on Android. The VPN is local: it only routes your phone’s DNS lookups (the step where an app asks “what is the address of ads.example.com?”) through the filter inside the app.

  • If the domain is on an enabled filter list or in your own rules, the app answers “does not exist” right away and the request never goes out.
  • Any other lookup is forwarded unchanged to a public DNS resolver (see below), and the answer is passed back to the app that asked.
  • The content of your traffic — pages, messages, video, downloads — is not routed through the filter and is never read.

The filter lists ship inside the app; filtering does not depend on a server of ours.

DNS resolvers

Lookups that are not blocked go to a public resolver, the same way they would without ShieldSpan. The resolver receives the domain name and your public IP address, under its own privacy terms:

ResolverAddressRole
Cloudflare DNS1.1.1.1Primary
Google Public DNS8.8.8.8Used only if Cloudflare doesn’t answer

These lookups use standard, unencrypted DNS, so your internet provider or network operator can see them, as they normally would.

What stays on your phone

To show you statistics and remember your choices, the app keeps the following in its private storage. None of it is sent to us.

Domains that were not blocked are counted but their names are not saved, so the app has no browsing history. On Android, ShieldSpan is excluded from system backups. On iOS, app data may be part of your own device or iCloud backup, which is controlled by Apple and you.

What we never collect

  • Browsing or DNS history
  • Name, email or phone number
  • IP address or location
  • Contacts, photos or files
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Analytics or usage events
  • Payment details
  • Content of your traffic

ShieldSpan has no accounts, in-app purchases or cloud sync. If you opt in to share diagnostics with Apple or Google, they may pass anonymous crash reports and aggregate usage figures to us through their developer consoles, under their own policies.

Sharing

Because we don’t receive personal data, we don’t sell it, share it or use it for advertising. The only third parties involved are the DNS resolvers above and Apple or Google as app store and operating system providers.

Deleting your data

  • Remove an allowed site or a rule from Filters → Allowed sites or Filters → My rules.
  • Turn protection off at any time from the Home tab or the system VPN settings.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all statistics and settings. On iPhone you can also remove the VPN profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) or the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data and to object to its processing. Since all ShieldSpan data stays on your device, you exercise these rights directly in the app. We hold no copy to hand over, and we do not sell or share personal information as the CCPA defines it. You can still contact us with any request and we will answer it.

Children

ShieldSpan is not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live. We don’t knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Filtering happens on the device and the app’s data lives in its private, sandboxed storage that other apps can’t read. Protect your phone with a passcode or biometric lock to keep that data safe.

Changes

If we change how ShieldSpan handles data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and point out important changes in the release notes or in the app.
