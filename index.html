01The basics

What does ShieldSpan block?

Advertising, in-app ads, trackers, analytics, error trackers, social buttons, cookie banners, phone maker telemetry and known malicious sites. Each group is a separate filter you can switch on or off in the Filters tab.

Blocking works on domain names, so it covers every app and browser on the phone, not just one.

Why does it need a VPN permission?

The VPN is how the system lets an app see which addresses your apps look up. ShieldSpan sets up a local VPN on the device: there is no remote server on the other end, and your traffic doesn’t leave the phone through us.

Lookups for blocked domains are answered on the spot with “does not exist”, so the ad never loads. Everything else is passed on to a public DNS resolver as usual.

How do I turn protection on or off?

Tap the shield on the Home tab. When it’s whole, protection is on; when the pieces drift apart, it’s off.

You can also switch it from the system settings — Settings → VPN on iPhone, or the VPN tile in quick settings on Android.

Can I block a site myself?

Yes. Open Filters → My rules and add a domain such as example.com. It will be blocked everywhere, on top of the built-in filter lists.

Does ShieldSpan slow down my connection or drain the battery?

Only domain lookups pass through the filter; downloads, video and page content go straight to the network. Because ads never load, many pages actually get lighter.

02Fixing problems

A site or app stopped working properly

Some services break when one of their tracking domains is blocked. Add the site under Filters → Allowed sites — for 1 hour, 24 hours or always — and ShieldSpan will stop blocking on it.

If you’re not sure which domain is to blame, open Statistics → Block log right after the problem happens and look at the most recent entries.

I still see some ads

Ads served from the same domain as the content itself — for example in some video and social apps — can’t be separated from the content by a domain filter.

Also check that the right filters are on in the Filters tab, and restart the app or browser after turning protection on: apps sometimes keep old lookups cached for a while.

Protection turns off by itself

Only one VPN can be active at a time. If you open another VPN app, the system switches ShieldSpan off — turn it back on when you’re done.

On Android, battery savers may stop background apps. Set ShieldSpan’s battery usage to Unrestricted, and turn on Launch with the system in ShieldSpan settings so protection comes back after a restart.

It says “Couldn’t turn on protection”

Usually the VPN permission was declined or removed. Tap the shield again and allow the connection request. If another VPN app is set as “always-on” in system settings, turn that off first.

03Your data

Can you see the sites I visit?

No. ShieldSpan has no servers and no accounts, and it doesn’t contain analytics or tracking SDKs. Statistics and the block log are kept on your phone only. Details are in our privacy policy.

Where do the statistics come from?

They’re counted on the device as the filter works: how many lookups were blocked, which categories and companies they belonged to and, on Android, which app made them. The app keeps the last 60 days.

How do I remove everything ShieldSpan stored?

Delete the app. All its statistics, rules and allowed sites are stored inside it and go away with it. On iPhone you can also remove the VPN profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
